# Boomwacht — Android Releases

This repository contains the official Android APK releases for **Boomwacht**, the AI-powered tree health monitoring and management platform.

---

## Download

Go to the [Releases](../../releases) tab and download the latest `Boomwacht.apk`.

---

## Installation

1. Download the APK from the **Releases** tab above
2. On your Android device, go to **Settings → Security → Install unknown apps**
3. Allow installation from your browser or file manager
4. Open the downloaded APK file and follow the on-screen instructions

> **Note:** Because Boomwacht is not yet listed on the Google Play Store, Android will show a warning about installing from an unknown source. This is normal — simply tap **Install anyway** to proceed.

## About Boomwacht

Boomwacht is a professional tree health monitoring and management platform built for:

- **Tree nurseries** — full inventory and growth monitoring
- **Municipalities** — public green space management with team reporting
- **Arborists & Horticulturists** — professional diagnosis and treatment records
- **Private users** — simple management of your own trees and garden

### Key features

- **AI disease detection** — photograph a leaf or bark and receive a diagnosis with confidence score and severity rating in seconds
- **Tree inventory** — manage hundreds of trees by species, location, health status, and growth measurements, with QR code support
- **Team management** — invite colleagues, assign roles, and collaborate in real time
- **Treatment plans** — AI-assisted treatment recommendations linked to detection results
- **Seasonal guides** — watering schedules, nutrition, and protection advice per season
- **Health reports** — automatically generated reports with trend analysis and forecasting
- **Expert consultation** — direct access to 500+ certified tree specialists, available 24/7 with an average response time of 15 minutes
